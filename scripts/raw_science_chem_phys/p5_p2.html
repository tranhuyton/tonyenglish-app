⚛️ 1. Cấu tạo Nguyên tử & Đồng vị (P5.1)

Thí nghiệm tán xạ hạt alpha của Rutherford:
• Observation 1: Most α-particles pass straight through undeflected ⇒ The atom is mostly empty space.
• Observation 2: A tiny fraction are deflected through large angles (> 90°) ⇒ The nucleus is extremely small, dense, and carries a concentrated positive charge.
Particle Location Relative Mass Relative Charge
Proton (p) Inside Nucleus 1 +1
Neutron (n) Inside Nucleus 1 0 (Neutral)
Electron (e) Orbiting in Shells 1 / 1840 (Negligible) −1
Ký hiệu Hạt nhân & Đồng vị:
• Nuclide Notation: AZX, where A = Nucleon number (total protons + neutrons) and Z = Proton / Atomic number (number of protons).
• Isotopes: Atoms of the same element having the same number of protons (same Z) but a different number of neutrons (different A). They have identical chemical properties because they have the same electron arrangement.

3 loại phóng xạ có bản chất, khối lượng, điện tích, khả năng ion hóa và độ đâm xuyên hoàn toàn khác biệt.

🔴 Alpha (α) Particle: Helium Nucleus (⁴₂He)

  • Composition: 2 protons and 2 neutrons (Charge: +2, Mass: 4 u).
  • Ionising Power: STRONGEST (due to large mass and +2 charge).
  • Penetrating Ability: WEAKEST (stopped by a single sheet of paper or ~5 cm of air).
  • Deflection: Deflected slightly by electric and magnetic fields (heavy mass).

🔵 Beta (β) Particle: Fast-Moving Electron (⁰₋₁e)

  • Composition: High-speed electron emitted from nucleus when a neutron decays into a proton (Charge: −1, Mass: 1/1840 u).
  • Ionising Power: MODERATE.
  • Penetrating Ability: MODERATE (passes through paper; stopped by ~5 mm of aluminium or ~1 m of air).
  • Deflection: Deflected strongly in opposite direction to alpha particles.

🟢 Gamma (γ) Ray: High-Energy EM Wave

  • Composition: High-frequency electromagnetic radiation (Charge: 0, Mass: 0).
  • Ionising Power: WEAKEST (passes through most atoms without interacting).
  • Penetrating Ability: STRONGEST (only attenuated by thick lead or several metres of concrete).
  • Deflection: NOT deflected by electric or magnetic fields (uncharged).

⏱️ 3. Phương trình Phân rã & Chu kỳ Bán rã

Alpha (α) Decay

AZX → A−4Z−2Y + ⁴₂He

Nucleon number A decreases by 4.
Proton number Z decreases by 2 (new chemical element formed).

Beta (β) Decay

AZX → AZ+1Y + ⁰−1e

A neutron inside nucleus transforms into a proton + electron.
Nucleon number A is unchanged; Proton number Z increases by 1.

⏱️ Half-Life (T½) Definition & Calculations

Half-life: The time taken for half the radioactive nuclei in any sample to decay, OR the time taken for the activity / count rate to halve.
• After 1 half-life → ½ remaining (50%)
• After 2 half-lives → ¼ remaining (25%)
• After 3 half-lives → ⅛ remaining (12.5%)
• After n half-lives → (½)n remaining.

🚨 CẢNH BÁO BẪY ĐỀ THI: Trừ Bức xạ Nền (Background Radiation)

Bức xạ nền luôn tồn tại trong môi trường tự nhiên (khí radon, tia vũ trụ, đá granite, thực phẩm).
• QUY TẮC SỐNG CÒN KHI LÀM BÀI TẬP: Luôn luôn phải trừ bức xạ nền trước khi chia đôi count rate: Corrected Count Rate = Measured Count Rate − Background! Chỉ lấy số đếm đã hiệu chỉnh đi chia đôi, sau đó dóng vào đồ thị để tìm chu kỳ bán rã.

🛡️ 4. Ứng dụng Thực tế & An toàn Phóng xạ

🏭 Ứng dụng trong Đời sống & Công nghiệp

  • Smoke Alarms (Alpha): Americium-241 ionises air particles. Smoke enters, absorbs alpha particles, current drops, sounding alarm. Safe because alpha cannot penetrate plastic casing.
  • Thickness Monitoring (Beta): Beta source placed above moving paper/foil sheet, detector below. If sheet gets thicker, fewer beta particles pass, computer adjusts roller pressure.
  • Medical Sterilisation & Cancer Therapy (Gamma): Gamma rays kill bacteria on packaged surgical instruments and destroy cancerous tumors.

⚠️ Quy tắc An toàn khi Tiếp xúc Phóng xạ

  • Use Long-Handled Tongs: Never touch radioactive sources directly with bare hands; increasing distance dramatically reduces dose received.
  • Point Away from Body: Never point sources at eyes or living tissue.
  • Store in Thick Lead Containers: Absorbs all alpha, beta, and most gamma radiation when not in use.
  • Wear Radiation Badges (Dosimeters): Monitors total cumulative exposure of radiation workers.
