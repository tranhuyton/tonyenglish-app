C10.2 Air quality and climate

🟦 CORE

  • 1. State the composition of clean, dry air as approximately 78% nitrogen, 21% oxygen, and the remainder as a mixture of noble gases and carbon dioxide.[cite: 3019]
  • 2. State the source of each of these air pollutants, limited to: (a) carbon dioxide (b) carbon monoxide and particulates (c) methane (d) oxides of nitrogen (e) sulfur dioxide.[cite: 3020]
  • 3. State the adverse effect of these air pollutants.[cite: 3034]
  • 4. State and explain strategies to reduce the effects of climate change: (a) planting trees (b) reduction in livestock farming (c) decreasing use of fossil fuels (d) increasing use of hydrogen and renewable energy.[cite: 3044]

🟩 SUPPLEMENT

  • 5. State and explain strategies to reduce the effects of acid rain: reducing emissions of sulfur dioxide by using low-sulfur fuels and flue gas desulfurisation with calcium oxide.[cite: 3052]
  • 6. Describe how the greenhouse gases carbon dioxide and methane cause global warming.[cite: 3053]
  • 7. Explain how oxides of nitrogen form in car engines and describe their removal by catalytic converters, limited to: 2CO + 2NO → 2CO₂ + N₂.[cite: 3056]
