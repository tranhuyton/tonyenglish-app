🌍 Day & Night (Rotation on Axis)

• Mechanism: The Earth rotates on its internal axis once every 24 hours (1 day) from west to east.
• The half facing the Sun experiences daylight; the half facing away experiences darkness (night).

🌕 Moon Orbit & Phases (Lunar Month)

• Mechanism: The Moon is Earth's natural satellite, completing one orbit in approximately 28 days (1 month).
• The Moon does not emit light; it reflects sunlight. As it orbits Earth, different proportions of its illuminated side face Earth, creating phases of the Moon.

☀️ Years & Seasons (Orbit & Tilt)

• Year: Earth completes one orbit around the Sun in 365.25 days (1 year).
• Seasons: Caused by the Earth's tilted axis (~23.5°). When a hemisphere is tilted towards the Sun, solar rays hit more directly and days are longer (Summer); when tilted away, rays spread out and days are shorter (Winter).

Structure of the Solar System

Category Planets Key Physical Characteristics
Rocky Planets (Inner) Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars Small, high density, solid rocky surfaces, few/no moons, close to Sun.
Asteroid Belt Between Mars and Jupiter Thousands of rocky and metallic remnants orbiting the Sun.
Gas / Ice Giants (Outer) Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune Huge diameter, low density, gaseous/liquid hydrogen and helium, extensive ring systems and many moons.

🚀 2. Orbital Speed & Gravitational Force (P6.1)

🧮 Orbital Speed Equation

v = 2πr / T
• v: Average orbital speed (m/s or km/s)
• r: Average orbital radius (distance from central body)
• 2πr: Circumference of circular orbit (distance travelled in one revolution)
• T: Orbital period (time taken for one complete orbit).
Effect of Distance on Gravitational Force and Speed:
• Gravitational attraction from the Sun decreases as distance increases.
• Planets further from the Sun experience smaller gravitational pull ⇒ they travel at slower orbital speeds and have longer orbital periods T. (e.g. Mercury: 88 days, Neptune: 165 years!).

🚨 CAMBRIDGE EXAM PITFALL: A Light-Year is a Unit of DISTANCE!

Despite containing the word "year", a light-year is NOT a unit of time! It is defined as the distance travelled by light through a vacuum in one Earth year (approximately 9.5 × 10¹⁵ m or 9.5 × 10¹² km).

✨ 3. Stars & The Universe (P6.2)

☀️ Nuclear Fusion in the Sun

The Sun is a medium-sized star powered by nuclear fusion in its core. Hydrogen nuclei fuse together under extreme temperature and gravitational pressure to form helium nuclei, releasing enormous energy:
4 ¹₁H → ⁴₂He + energy.
In a stable main-sequence star, inward gravitational pull is exactly balanced by outward radiation thermal pressure.

🌌 Life Cycle of Stars: Sun-like vs Massive Stars

⭐ Sun-like Star (Low / Medium Mass)

  1. Interstellar Cloud / Nebula: Dust and hydrogen gas collapse under gravity.
  2. Protostar: Heats up until fusion ignites.
  3. Stable Main Sequence Star: Billions of years fusing hydrogen.
  4. Red Giant: Core runs out of hydrogen, outer layers expand and cool.
  5. Planetary Nebula: Outer gas envelope ejected into space.
  6. White Dwarf → Black Dwarf: Hot dense core cools over billions of years.

💥 Massive Star (High Mass)

  1. Nebula → Massive Protostar
  2. Massive Main Sequence Star: Burns fuel very rapidly (millions of years).
  3. Red Supergiant: Expands massively, fusing heavier elements up to iron.
  4. Supernova: Cataclysmic explosion dispersing heavy elements across the cosmos.
  5. Neutron Star OR Black Hole: Extremely dense core collapses into either a neutron star or, for the most massive stars, a black hole from which even light cannot escape.

🔭 The Milky Way & The Expanding Universe (Big Bang)

• The Milky Way: A spiral galaxy containing billions of stars, measuring approximately 100,000 light-years in diameter.
• Redshift: Light from distant galaxies is shifted towards the longer wavelength (red) end of the spectrum. The further away a galaxy is, the greater its redshift and the faster it is moving away from us.
• Evidence for Big Bang: Redshift proves space itself is expanding in all directions, confirming that the universe originated from a single hot, dense point ~13.8 billion years ago.
