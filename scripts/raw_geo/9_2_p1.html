9.2 The impact of globalisation and the role of transnational corporations

1. Globalisation and its key features

Globalisation describes the rapidly increasing and more complex global links that have developed since the 1960s. These links are economic, cultural, and political.

2. What is a Transnational Corporation (TNC)?

A TNC is a huge company that operates in multiple countries. There are over 80,000 TNCs worldwide, and the top 10 have revenues exceeding the GDP of many sovereign nations!

4. Impacts of TNCs

Impacts on Host Countries (LICs/NICs):
  • (+) Job creation & Skills: Provides thousands of jobs and brings in new technology (Technology Transfer).
  • (+) Multiplier effect: Local businesses grow to support the TNC.
  • (-) Exploitation: Risk of sweatshops, long hours, and poor safety.
  • (-) Economic Leakage: Most profits go back to the HIC (repatriation of profits).
  • (-) Footloose nature: They can close factories and move to a cheaper country overnight.
