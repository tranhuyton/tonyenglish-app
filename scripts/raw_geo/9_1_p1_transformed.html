9.1 Changing employment structures

1. The Four Economic Sectors

Nghe phần này

In all modern economies, jobs are divided into four broad economic sectors. As a country develops, the proportion of people working in each sector changes significantly.

Primary Sector (e.g., Dairy farming in New Zealand)
Extracts raw materials from the earth or sea (farming, fishing, mining).

Secondary Sector (e.g., Grain processing in USA)
Manufactures raw materials into finished products (factories, construction).

Tertiary Sector (e.g., Trans-Siberian Railway transport)
Provides services to people and businesses (retail, transport, banking).

Quaternary Sector (e.g., Research scientist)
Provides high-tech information, research, and expertise (biotech, aerospace).
