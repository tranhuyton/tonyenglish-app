Activity 11

  • 1. Outline two ways of controlling lava flows.
  • 2. Outline two ways in which volcanoes can be predicted.
💡 View Suggested Answers
  • 1. 1) Spraying lava with seawater to cool and solidify it; 2) Constructing earthen dams or barriers to divert the flow away from settlements.
  • 2. 1) Monitoring seismic activity for small tremors caused by rising magma; 2) Using GPS to measure ground deformation as the volcano swells.

Activity 12

  • 1. Identify ways in which earthquakes can be predicted.
  • 2. Explain the term 'safe house'. Briefly explain how houses can be made 'safe'.
💡 View Suggested Answers
  • 1. Predicting earthquakes exactly is difficult, but methods include monitoring crustal movement and strain along faults, tracking seismic gaps, and monitoring radon gas emissions.
  • 2. A 'safe house' is designed to withstand earthquake shaking. They can be made safe by incorporating features like deep foundations, shock absorbers, reinforced walls, and lightweight roofs.

Activity 13

  • 1. Explain why earthquakes occur in Haiti.
  • 2. Suggest how government actions after the 2021 earthquake helped manage the recovery process.
💡 View Suggested Answers
  • 1. Haiti is located on a microplate squeezed between the North American and Caribbean plates. The movement and friction along these boundaries cause stress, leading to earthquakes.
  • 2. Government actions helped by mobilizing Municipal Civil Protection Committees, conducting rapid damage assessments, and achieving greater coordination among groups working in disaster risk management.

Activity 14

  • 1. Explain the impacts of volcanic activity on Mount Etna.
  • 2. Analyse ways in which the threats posed by Mount Etna have been managed.
💡 View Suggested Answers
  • 1. Mount Etna's eruptions have destroyed tourist infrastructure, threatened settlements, closed airports due to volcanic ash, and disrupted the local economy, though human fatalities have been relatively low.
  • 2. Threats have been managed by building earth barriers and using concrete blocks to divert lava flows, monitoring changes in volcano shape and temperature, tracking radon gas, and providing financial aid to affected workers.
