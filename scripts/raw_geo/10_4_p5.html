Test your understanding of this topic

Activity 1

  • 1 Explain the difference between renewable and non-renewable energy sources.
  • 2 What do you understand by the terms 'energy mix' and 'clean air transition'?
  • 3 Briefly discuss two reasons why countries are keen to develop renewable sources of energy.
💡 View Suggested Answers
  • 1 Renewable energy sources can be replenished naturally (e.g., solar, wind), while non-renewable sources are finite and will run out (e.g., coal, oil).
  • 2 'Energy mix' refers to the combination of different energy sources used by a country. 'clean air transition' is the shift from polluting fossil fuels to cleaner energy sources to reduce emissions.
  • 3 To improve energy security by reducing reliance on imported fossil fuels, and to mitigate climate change by cutting greenhouse gas emissions.
