Activity 3

  • 1 Define the Green Revolution.
  • 2 Discuss three advantages and three disadvantages of Green Revolution farming.
  • 3 In what ways is the New Green Revolution introducing new farming practices?
💡 View Suggested Answers
  • 1 The Green Revolution refers to agricultural improvements post-1960 utilizing High Yielding Varieties (HYVs), fertilizers, and irrigation to boost production.
  • 2 Advantages: Higher yields, varied diets, improved infrastructure. Disadvantages: High chemical input costs, environmental degradation, increased rural inequality.
  • 3 The New Green Revolution introduces practices like breeding drought/salt-tolerant crops, improving nutritional value (biofortification), and integrated pest management (IPM).

Activity 5

  • 1 How has globalisation impacted traditional agricultural production in many parts of the world?
  • 2 a Define food miles.
  • b Why are food miles an important environmental concept?
💡 View Suggested Answers
  • 1 Globalisation has shifted focus to export-oriented cash crops, increased monoculture, and made local farmers vulnerable to global market fluctuations.
  • 2 a Food miles are the distance food travels from farm to consumer.
  • b It is an important concept because longer distances mean higher carbon emissions from transport, impacting climate change.
