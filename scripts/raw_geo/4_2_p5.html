    b) All types of plate boundaries (divergent, convergent, and conservative).

Activity 6

  • 1. Describe the distribution of shallow- and deep-focus earthquakes.
  • 2. Distinguish between body waves and surface waves.
💡 View Suggested Answers
  • 1. Shallow-focus earthquakes occur at all plate boundaries and are close to the surface. Deep-focus earthquakes are mostly associated with subduction zones (convergent boundaries) where a plate is forced deep into the mantle.
  • 2. Body waves travel through the Earth's interior (e.g., P-waves and S-waves), whereas surface waves travel along the Earth's surface and typically cause more destruction.

Activity 7

  • 1. Distinguish between shield and cone volcanoes.
  • 2. Identify the types of plate boundaries associated with shield and cone volcanoes.
💡 View Suggested Answers
  • 1. Shield volcanoes are gently sloping and formed by hot, runny basaltic lava. Cone volcanoes are steep-sided and formed by thicker, less fluid magma and loose pyroclastic fragments.
  • 2. Shield volcanoes are typically associated with divergent boundaries and hotspots. Cone volcanoes are associated with convergent/destructive boundaries.

Activity 8

  • 1. Distinguish between a volcano's crater and its chamber.
  • 2. Explain three hazards related to volcanic activity.
💡 View Suggested Answers
  • 1. The magma chamber is a large underground reservoir of magma beneath the volcano. The crater is the surface depression surrounding the main vent.
  • 2. Three hazards include: 1) Lava flows that destroy infrastructure; 2) Pyroclastic flows (fast-moving hot gas and ash) that cause instant destruction; 3) Ash clouds that damage crops and disrupt air travel.
