Activity 1

Figure 5.11 Global sea level change projections from 1800 to 2100
  • 1. Compare the greenhouse effect and the enhanced greenhouse effect.
  • 2. Using Figure 5.11 (page 116), explain why there are variations in the predicted sea level rise by 2100.
💡 View Suggested Answers
  • 1. The natural greenhouse effect is a vital process where atmospheric gases trap heat, keeping the Earth warm enough to sustain life. In contrast, the enhanced greenhouse effect is caused by human activities (such as burning fossil fuels and deforestation) that increase the concentration of these gases, trapping excess heat and leading to dangerous global climate change.
  • 2. Variations in predicted sea level rise depend on different future greenhouse gas emission scenarios. High-emissions scenarios assume continued heavy reliance on fossil fuels, resulting in greater global warming, more thermal expansion of seawater, and increased melting of ice sheets. Low-emissions scenarios assume significant global mitigation efforts, leading to less warming and a smaller overall sea level rise.
