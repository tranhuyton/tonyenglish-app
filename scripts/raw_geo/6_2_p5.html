  • 2. What do you understand by the term 'demographic dividend'?
  • 3. a) Define the term 'sex ratio'. b) State two ways in which the 'natural' sex ratio can be distorted.
💡 View Suggested Answers
  • 1. Niger (Stage 2) has a very wide base indicating a high youth dependency ratio and high birth rate, but a narrow top. Japan (Stage 5) has a narrow base showing a small youth population, but a wide top indicating a massive elderly dependency ratio and high life expectancy.

  • 3. a) The ratio of males to females in a population. b) It can be distorted by gender-selective migration (e.g. male migrant workers) or cultural practices like sex-selective abortion.

Activity 2

Table 6.9 India: The decline of the crude birth rate, 1960–2022
  • 1. Study Table 6.9. Discuss the reasons behind the steady decline of India's birth rate.
  • 2. Why have some academics stressed the importance of India utilising its gender dividend?
💡 View Suggested Answers
  • 1. India's birth rate has steadily declined due to improved female literacy, urbanization, widespread availability of family planning, and rising costs of raising children.
  • 2. Academics stress the gender dividend because increasing the historically low workforce participation of India's large female population would dramatically boost productivity and economic growth.
