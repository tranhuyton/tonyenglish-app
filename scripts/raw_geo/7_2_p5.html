Activities (Block 1)

  • 2. Identify the problems of waste management in urban environments.
  • 3. Suggest reasons why housing can be an issue in urban areas.

Activities (Block 2)

  • 4. Identify three measures or pieces of evidence of inequality within Rio de Janeiro.
  • 5. Suggest why there is a housing shortage in Rio de Janeiro.
  • 6. Outline the opportunities of living in squatter settlements.
  • 7. Distinguish between settlements that are called 'slums of hope' and those that are called 'slums of despair'.

Activities (Block 3)

  • 8. Explain reasons for the growth of developments in the rural-to-urban fringe.
  • 9. Suggest how developments in the rural-to-urban fringe impact the natural environment.
💡 View Suggested Answers

Activities (Block 1) Answers

  • 1. Opportunities include better employment prospects and higher incomes, access to a wider range of services (healthcare, education, retail), a diverse range of cultural and leisure facilities, and well-developed public transport networks.
  • 2. Problems include the vast quantities of waste generated, the rapid filling up of landfill sites, high costs of disposal, and the environmental impacts such as greenhouse gas emissions (methane) and potential contamination from unmanaged waste.
  • 3. Housing is an issue due to massive demand caused by rapid rural-to-urban migration, population growth, longer life expectancies, and an increase in single-person households. This leads to housing shortages, high costs, overcrowding, and the growth of informal squatter settlements.

Activities (Block 2) Answers

  • 4. Evidence of inequality includes: 1) The stark contrast between wealthy gated communities and poorly serviced favelas. 2) The large income disparity illustrated by a Lorenz curve. 3) The difference in service provision between gentrified areas (like Woodstock) and relocation camps or squatter areas.
  • 5. The housing shortage is driven by rapid urbanisation that outpaces the government's ability to build affordable homes. Poorer migrants are unable to afford formal housing due to high land prices, forcing them into informal settlements.
  • 6. Squatter settlements can provide affordable housing without formal rent, close proximity to city jobs (reducing commute times), a strong sense of community, and the potential for incremental upgrading as residents' incomes improve.
  • 7. 'Slums of hope' are typically more central informal settlements where residents have better access to employment, allowing them to earn incomes and improve their homes. 'Slums of despair' are located on the city outskirts with limited job access and fewer prospects for upgrading.

Activities (Block 3) Answers

  • 8. Developments occur on the fringe due to the availability of cheaper, more extensive land, excellent accessibility via ring roads and motorways, ample space for parking, and a cleaner, less congested environment compared to the inner city.
  • 9. They can negatively impact the environment by eroding agricultural land (urban sprawl), destroying natural habitats and reducing biodiversity, increasing localized air pollution from extra traffic, and replacing permeable surfaces with impermeable concrete, which can increase flood risks.
