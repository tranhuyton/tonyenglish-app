Activity 1

  • 1. Calculate for Manaus:
    • a. the mean average monthly temperature
    • b. the annual precipitation.
  • 2. Compare the seasonal variations in rainfall and sunshine (hours) for Manaus.
💡 View Suggested Answers
  • 1a. The mean average monthly temperature for Manaus is consistently high, around 27°C to 28°C throughout the year.
  • 1b. The annual precipitation for Manaus is very high, typically around 2,100 mm to 2,300 mm.
  • 2. While rainfall is high year-round, Manaus has a distinct wetter season and a relatively drier season. During the wetter months, sunshine hours decrease due to heavy convective cloud cover. In contrast, during the drier months, sunshine hours are generally higher because there is less cloud cover blocking the sun.

Activity 2

Figure 3.15 Stratification and food web in a tropical rainforest
  • 1. Suggest how the vegetation in Figure 3.15 is adapted to conditions in the rainforest.
  • 2. Explain how animals are adapted to living in tropical rainforests.
💡 View Suggested Answers
  • 1. Rainforest vegetation adapts by having large, broad leaves to maximize sunlight capture in the dense canopy, "drip tips" to quickly shed heavy rainfall and prevent rot, and buttress roots for structural stability in shallow, nutrient-poor soils.
  • 2. Animals adapt to the rainforest by being arboreal (tree-dwelling) with prehensile tails for climbing (e.g., monkeys), utilizing camouflage to hide from predators or ambush prey (e.g., stick insects, jaguars), and developing loud vocalizations to communicate through dense foliage (e.g., howler monkeys).
