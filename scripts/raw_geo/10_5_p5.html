Figure 10.31 Actions, consequences and impacts
  • 1 Explain the terms:
  • a Energy gap
  • b Energy security.
  • 2 Write a summary of Figure 10.31 (page 277).
  • 3 Why do many countries hold reserves of energy resources?
  • 4 What are energy pathways and why is their security so important?
  • 5 With reference to Table 10.9, examine the physical factors that can affect the production of energy in a country.
💡 View Suggested Answers
  • 1 a Energy gap is the difference between a country's rising demand for energy and its domestic production.
  • b Energy security is the uninterrupted availability of energy sources at an affordable price.
  • 2 The figure likely details global energy trade flows, highlighting major exporters like the Middle East and importers like Europe/Asia.
  • 3 To ensure short-term energy security in case of supply disruptions (e.g., geopolitical conflicts or natural disasters).
  • 4 Energy pathways are the routes (pipelines, shipping lanes) energy travels from producer to consumer. Their security is vital to prevent supply interruptions.
  • 5 Physical factors include the presence of fossil fuel reserves, suitable climate/relief for renewables (e.g., high insolation for solar, valleys for HEP), and vulnerability to natural hazards.
