Activities

  • 1. Define the term 'urbanisation'.
  • 2. Describe the cycle of urbanisation.
  • 3. Explain briefly the causes of rapid urban growth.
💡 View Suggested Answers

Activities Answers

  • 1. Urbanisation is the process by which an increasing proportion of a country's population comes to live in towns and cities.
  • 2. The cycle of urbanisation involves four main stages: Urbanisation (the initial movement into urban areas); Suburbanisation (outward growth of towns and cities to engulf surrounding rural areas); Counterurbanisation (movement of population from large urban areas to smaller urban areas and rural settlements); and Reurbanisation (the regeneration of inner urban areas causing people to move back).
  • 3. Causes include economic pull factors (better employment prospects), social pull factors (better healthcare and education), economic/physical push factors (poor farming incomes, natural hazards in rural areas), a higher rate of natural increase (due to a youthful urban population), and the multiplier effect from new industrial developments.
