9.3 Tourism is a growing industry

1. The Global Growth of Tourism

International tourism has grown exponentially since the 1950s. In 1950, there were just 25 million international tourist arrivals. By 2019 (pre-COVID), this figure had reached nearly 1.5 billion!

3. Types of Tourism

  • Mass tourism: Tens of thousands of people going to the same resort (sun & beaches). Ex: Benidorm, Phuket.
  • Ecotourism: Small-scale, nature-based, minimizes impact, benefits locals. Ex: Costa Rica, Galapagos.
  • Adventure tourism: Extreme sports in remote environments. Ex: Queenstown (NZ).
  • Cultural tourism: Visiting heritage sites. Ex: Kyoto, Rome.

4. Impacts of Tourism (EVE framework)

Economic

(+) Jobs, foreign exchange, infrastructure.

(-) Economic Leakage (profits return to HICs), seasonal low-wage jobs, local inflation.

Social & Cultural

(+) Preserves heritage, improves language skills.

(-) Demonstration effect (loss of culture), Overtourism, crowding, crime.

Environmental

(+) Entry fees fund conservation.

(-) Carbon footprint, coral reef destruction, water overuse, litter.

5. Sustainable Tourism

Definition: Tourism that meets the needs of present tourists and host communities while protecting and enhancing opportunities for the future.
